**Lumen Orientale** is an independent English-language journal about paying
attention.

The name brings together *lumen*, a measure of light, and *orientale*, a nod to
the east and to beginnings. It is a reminder that fresh perspective often
arrives quietly: with the first cup of coffee, on an unfamiliar street, or in
the middle of making something by hand.

Here you will find thoughtful, unhurried writing on creativity, everyday
rituals, places, and the small details that give ordinary days their light.
Everything is written to be read slowly and shared generously.

## A note to readers

This journal is a work in progress. If a story gives you an idea, a little calm,
or a reason to notice something new, it has done what it came here to do.
