## Write for us!

Have an article, reflection, or story you would like to share? Lumen Orientale welcomes contributions from readers who wish to share their knowledge, experiences, and perspectives on the Catholic faith, sacred liturgy, Church history, and the life of the Church.

If you would like to contribute an article for publication on our website, we would be delighted to hear from you. Please get in touch with us through our contact page to discuss your submission.

Together, let us continue to share the truth and beauty of our Catholic faith.

Feel free to send your articles to our Editor via email at [poonlhchavez@gmail.com](mailto:poonlhchavez@gmail.com).

We review every article submission, though we cannot guarantee a reply or publication.
