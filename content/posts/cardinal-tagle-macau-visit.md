**MACAU, CHINA** -- Cardinal Luis Antonio Gokim Tagle, the newly elected Dean of the College of Cardinals, is set to visit Macau for celebrations marking the 450th anniversary of the establishment of the Diocese of Macau, according to a report by the Macau Diocese's local publication [O Clarim](https://oclarim.com.mo/article/luis-antonio-tagle-%E9%A1%9E%E6%80%9D-%E5%AE%89%E5%A4%9A%E5%B0%BC-%E5%A1%94%E6%A0%BC%E8%90%8A-%E6%A8%9E%E6%A9%9F%E8%92%9E%E8%87%A8%E6%BE%B3%E9%96%80-%E6%A8%9E%E6%A9%9F%E5%9C%98%E6%96%B0%E4%BB%BB%E5%9C%98%E9%95%B7%E5%B0%87%E5%9C%A8%E6%BE%B3%E9%96%80%E4%B8%BB%E7%A6%AE%E5%A4%A7%E7%A6%AE%E5%BD%8C%E6%92%92?lang=zh).

Cardinal Tagle was recently elected Dean of the College of Cardinals on 8th October 2026. His visit coincides with a significant milestone in the history of the Catholic Church in Macau, whose diocese was established in 1576.

According to O Clarim, Cardinal Tagle will preside over the Solemn Pontifical Mass of Thanksgiving in Macau on 31st October 2026, marking the 450th anniversary of the establishment of the Diocese of Macau and celebrating its rich missionary heritage.

## A Historic Diocese in East Asian Region

![](https://cbcpnews.net/cbcpnews/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Macau-Cathedral.jpg)

Established on 23rd January 1576, by Pope Gregory XIII through the apostolic constitution *Super specula militantis Ecclesiæ*, the Diocese of Macau is among the historic ecclesiastical jurisdictions of East Asian Region.

For centuries, Macau served as an important centre for Catholic missionary activity in Asia. Missionaries travelled from the territory to China, Japan, and other parts of the region, contributing to the spread of the Gospel and the development of ecclesiastical, educational, and cultural institutions.

The diocese's 450th anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on this missionary heritage and the enduring contribution of the Catholic Church to the religious and cultural history of Macau and the wider region.

## Milestone for the Universal Church

The visit of Cardinal Tagle, now Dean of the College of Cardinals, adds particular significance to the anniversary celebrations. As one of the prominent figures in the Catholic Church's missionary work, he has served as Pro-Prefect of the Dicastery for Evangelization, which is responsible for promoting and supporting the Church's evangelizing mission.

The anniversary also invites the faithful of Macau to give thanks for the generations of bishops, priests, religious, and lay Catholics who have sustained the life of the local Church since its foundation.

As the Diocese of Macau celebrates 450 years of Catholic faith, the milestone serves as a reminder of its historical role in the evangelization of Asia and its continuing mission to proclaim the Gospel in the contemporary world.
